Normative outcome: Approve those outbound-capture defaults for Wix, Webflow, Framer and Durable: each fresh crawl requires current native authority, the exact controlled origin and explicit purpose; DNS proof establishes origin control only, not ownership/license of every asset. ### RL-102 — Newsletter collection requires a real destination

Date: 2026-10-28 · Ruler: ct · Governs: PAGE-013

Question: May an unconnected newsletter popup refuse address collection until its authorized audience integration exists?

Default adopted/proposed: Offers/announcements work; no newsletter address collection or automatic subscription before LINK-003 and an authorized audience.

Outcome: **adopt with rider** — Approve the proposed default subject to the precise conditions below.

Normative outcome: Approve failing closed until a real authorized audience exists; the completed newsletter action must disclose its destination, obtain the visitor’s explicit signup and applicable verification, report the actual provider outcome and honor suppression. Offers and announcements remain independent; newsletter signup remains a required capability.

Why and incumbent comparison: Squarespace distinguishes newsletter signup from a call-to-action popup and requires explicit storage plus subscription verification. Refusing to collect addresses into an absent destination follows that configured-destination behavior. The current missing integration is an engineering dependency, not policy permission to delete newsletter scope.

Placement (one copy): State the provider-bound newsletter guard once in LINK-003 and cite it from PAGE-013.

Engineering/proof: Authorized audience access may be supplied later; no account purchase or marketing consent is inferred. LINK-003 implementation and newsletter journey remain open.
